About

I got into programming by making small automation scripts, and got into security by accidentally breaking one of my own projects and wanting to know why. Lately I've also been diving into AI/ML, mostly how models can be used for security tooling, and where they introduce new attack surface of their own. Since then I've been pulled in a few directions, I like building products end to end, picking them apart to find where the assumptions fail, and now exploring what AI changes about both.

Right now I'm part of my university's cyber student activity unit, working through osint and web exploitation challenges, and building side projects to get more comfortable with real infrastructure: auth flows, APIs, and the small mistakes that turn into big problems. Longer term I'm aiming for a role where I can do both: build things and help keep them safe.
